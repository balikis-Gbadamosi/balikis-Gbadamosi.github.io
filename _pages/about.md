I am a Master's student in Sociology at Kent State University, 
specializing in Medical and Mental Health Sociology. My research 
sits at the intersection of trauma, adversity, and health — 
exploring how life experiences shape mental and physical health 
outcomes across the life course.

My core research interests include:
- Medical and Mental Health Sociology
- The Stress Process Model
- Adverse Childhood Experiences (ACEs)
- Trauma and resilience
- Life course theory

I am passionate about understanding how structural and social 
factors contribute to health disparities, and how early 
adversity leaves lasting imprints on individual wellbeing.
