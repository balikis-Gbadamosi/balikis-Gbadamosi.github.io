KinAndAge is a curated digital library and resource hub focused on 
African aging, kinship caregiving, and intergenerational relationships. 
It brings together scholarly resources, research summaries, and 
accessible content on how aging is experienced across African contexts.

## What KinAndAge Does

Serves as a well-organised scholarly resource for researchers, 
students, and practitioners interested in:
- African gerontology
- Kinship and intergenerational care
- Mental health and aging in African communities
- The intersection of trauma and later life outcomes

## Purpose
KinAndAge fills a gap in easily accessible, Africa-centered resources 
on aging and family care — centralizing knowledge that is often 
scattered across disciplines and databases.
