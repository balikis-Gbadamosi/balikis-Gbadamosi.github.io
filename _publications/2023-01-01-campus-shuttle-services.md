This paper investigates the operational challenges and future prospects 
of university campus shuttle services at Lagos State University, Nigeria. 
Drawing on qualitative data collected from 20 respondents including 
shuttle operators and students, the study identifies key challenges such 
as mechanical issues, fuel scarcity, scheduling conflicts, and 
overcrowding, and their impact on service efficiency. The paper proposes 
actionable recommendations including increasing shuttle frequency and 
improving communication efforts for students.
